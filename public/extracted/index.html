1. Eligibility. One account per person and per device. Duplicate, fake or automated accounts are banned and their balance is cancelled.

3. Team commission. Commission is credited on up to 3 levels and only after a referred member has watched 20 ads. Self-referral is not allowed.

5. Fair use. VPNs, bots, scripts or any attempt to manipulate timers or rewards lead to a permanent ban.

6. Privacy. We store your name, email, device identifier and earning activity to run the service and prevent fraud. We never sell your personal data. Contact us through our WhatsApp channel to request deletion.
